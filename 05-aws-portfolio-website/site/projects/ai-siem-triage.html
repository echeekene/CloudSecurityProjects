AI Augmented SIEM Threat Detection & Triage

An end to end pipeline that detects a simulated brute force attack in Splunk and uses AI to draft the incident triage.

  • Splunk Enterprise
  • SPL
  • Python
  • Generative AI
  • MITRE ATT&CK
  • HEC

Goal

Show a complete detection and triage flow: synthetic logs, Splunk detection, Python processing, AI analysis, and a Splunk dashboard.

Scenario

A brute force attack: many failed logins from one IP followed by a successful login, hidden among realistic noise such as legitimate users mistyping passwords.

Design decisions

  • Detection: an SPL search flags any source IP with at least 10 failures plus at least 1 success.
  • Deterministic scoring: risk scores are computed in Python, kept separate from the AI generated narrative so results stay explainable and debuggable.
  • Constrained AI: MITRE ATT&CK suggestions are limited to a validated list of candidate techniques in the prompt.
  • Closing the loop: AI results are written back to Splunk through HEC into an ai_triage index for dashboarding.

Status

The log generator and detection query are built. Next up is the Python receiver that processes the Splunk alert and routes it to AI analysis.
